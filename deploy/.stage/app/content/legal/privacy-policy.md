## What we collect {#what-we-collect}

### Information you give us

- **Mobile number** — you sign in with a one-time code (OTP) sent to it. It identifies your account; there is no password.
- **Name** and, if you choose to add it, your **email address**.
- **Delivery addresses** — contact name and phone for the address, house and street, landmark, city, state and pincode. You can save several and remove them at any time.
- **Order details** — what you order, prices, offers and coupons used, delivery slot, delivery instructions and order notes you type.
- **Ratings and reviews** you write about products or deliveries.
- **Support requests** — the messages you send us and the order they are about.
- **A referral code** if you enter one when signing up.

### Information created when you use the service

- **Your account record** — when it was created, when you last signed in, your notification choices, your referral code, wallet balance and loyalty points, and the history of every wallet and points change.
- **Order and delivery records** — each status change of your order and who made it, the four-digit handover code that confirms delivery, the name and phone number of the store employee delivering it, and the time it was delivered.
- **Payment records** — for online payments, the payment method, amount, status and the payment provider's reference numbers. We do **not** receive or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login; you enter those directly with the payment provider.
- **Notifications** we send you inside the app or website, and a log of WhatsApp order updates (with your number masked).
- **Your cart and wishlist.**

### What we do not collect

The shopping app does not ask for your device location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone or files, and we do not use advertising identifiers, analytics or advertising SDKs, or tracking across other companies' apps and websites. Your delivery location is the address and pincode you type.

## How we use it {#how-we-use-it}

- To create and secure your account and sign you in (OTP).
- To show prices, stock and delivery options for your pincode, take your order, deliver it and collect payment.
- To process cancellations, returns and refunds, including credits to your wallet.
- To run the loyalty points, wallet, offers and Refer & Earn programmes you take part in.
- To send you messages about your orders (in the app or website, and by WhatsApp or SMS where switched on).
- To send offers and reminders such as a forgotten cart — only while "Offers" is switched on in your notification settings.
- To answer support requests and resolve complaints.
- To prevent fraud and misuse (for example, limits on login codes and referral checks) and to keep records the law requires (tax invoices, accounts).

We use your personal data for these purposes on the basis of your consent given when you sign up and place orders, and, where the law allows it, for legitimate uses such as complying with a legal obligation.

## Who we share it with {#sharing}

We do not sell your personal data. We share only what each party needs:

- **Our delivery staff** — the store's own employees who deliver your order see your name, the delivery phone number, the address and your delivery instructions while the order is on their run. After delivery their app no longer shows your contact details.
- **Payment provider** — when you pay online, Razorpay processes the payment. It receives the amount and the details you enter on its payment screen; we may pre-fill your name, phone number and email to save you typing. Razorpay handles that data under its own privacy policy.
- **Messaging providers** — the SMS service that delivers login codes receives your mobile number and the code. If WhatsApp order updates are switched on, Meta's WhatsApp Business service receives your number and the order update.
- **Hosting and storage providers** — our servers and database are run by cloud providers on our behalf. Product and brand images are served by Cloudinary; your device fetches them from Cloudinary's servers, which see your device's internet (IP) address like any website does.
- **Authorities** — when the law requires it, for example tax authorities or a valid order from a court or law enforcement agency.

Our hosting providers are: [[HOSTING_PROVIDERS]].

## How long we keep it {#retention}

- **Account details** (name, phone, email, addresses, preferences, cart, wishlist, notifications, reviews): until you delete your account — see [Account deletion & data retention](/account-deletion).
- **Orders, invoices, payments, refunds, and the wallet and points ledgers**: kept after account deletion, without your name and contact details, for as long as tax and accounting laws require (currently [[FINANCIAL_RECORD_RETENTION]]) and to deal with disputes and chargebacks.
- **Closed support requests**: kept without your name and phone number as a record of complaints and how they were resolved.
- **Login codes**: deleted automatically within about an hour of expiring.
- **Records of who changed what in our admin system**: kept for security and audit; a customer's details in these records are removed when the account is deleted.

## Security {#security}

All traffic between the apps, the website and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Login codes are stored only in hashed form and expire after five minutes; staff passwords are hashed. Access to customer data inside the store's admin system depends on the staff member's role, and changes are logged. Delivery staff see a customer's contact details only for orders assigned to them, while on the run. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Your choices and rights {#your-rights}

- **See and correct your details** — edit your name and email in your profile, and add, change or remove addresses at any time.
- **Notifications** — switch order messages, offers, SMS, WhatsApp and push messages on or off in Notifications.
- **Withdraw consent and delete your account** — from Account → Legal & about → Delete my account in the app, or on the [account deletion page](/account-deletion) of the website. Deletion is immediate once nothing is pending.
- **Ask about your data** — for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, or anything this policy does not answer, write to us (see "Contact and grievances"). We will respond within the time the law allows.
- **Nominate someone** to exercise these rights for you if you are unable to, by writing to us.
- **Complain** — to us first; if you are not satisfied, Indian law gives you the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Children {#children}

The service is meant for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account and place orders. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Cookies and storage on your device {#device-storage}

The website and apps store a small amount of information on your device to keep you signed in and remember your pincode and cart. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Details: [Cookies & local storage](/cookies-storage).
